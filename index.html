Questions, answered

Is there a minimum quantity?

Most pickups need at least 10 kg in total. E-waste and appliances can be picked up individually.

How do you weigh my scrap?

With a certified digital scale, placed where you can see the reading.

How do I get paid?

Instantly, by UPI or cash, once you approve the weight and amount.

Do you charge for pickup?

No. Pickup is free within our service areas.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp at least 2 hours before your slot.
